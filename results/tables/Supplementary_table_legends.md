## Table S1. Secondary-structure elements and strand pairings

(A) Principal secondary-structure elements assigned using DSSP.
E1–E4 and H1–H2 are numbered in polypeptide-chain order.
Boundaries use the original PDB residue numbering.

(B) Geometric contacts between adjacent sheet strands.
Contact counts represent interstrand Cα atom pairs separated by
less than 5.5 Å, not hydrogen-bond counts. Minimum distances are
the closest interstrand Cα separations. Axis cosines describe
the relative directions of the strand axes; negative values
indicate opposing directions. All five structures have the
sheet order E1–E4–E2–E3, with all three adjacencies antiparallel.

DSSP assignments were mapped by chain, residue number and
insertion code. Residues omitted by DSSP were treated as coil
for segmentation. In full-chain Dbh, residue A35 was omitted;
it lies outside the analyzed little-finger domain.

## Table S2. Pairwise structural comparisons

Sixteen TM-align comparisons include all ten unique pairs among
the five analyzed structures and six comparisons of designed
proteins against full natural polymerase chains. Both query-
and target-length-normalized TM-scores are reported for each
alignment; these are two normalizations of the same alignment.

Aligned length is the number of aligned residue pairs.
RMSD is calculated over the aligned residues. Sequence identity
is the percentage of identical residues among aligned pairs,
not global sequence identity. Domain and full-chain comparisons
are distinguished explicitly.

The natural-to-natural comparison is Pol IV LF versus Dbh LF:
95 aligned residues, RMSD 1.91 Å, sequence identity 11.6%, and
TM-scores 0.737 and 0.780. The two TM-scores reflect different
length normalizations, not an uncertainty interval.
