Figure S1. Structure-guided sequence comparison of designed proteins and natural little-finger domains. The four frozen pairwise TM-align alignments were combined using Pol IV LF (4R8U:A, residues 233–341) as the common reference. Rows show LNG, XM2H, AM2M, Pol IV LF and Dbh LF; dashes indicate alignment gaps. Bars mark the principal secondary-structure elements defined in Table S1A: strands E1–E4 are blue, green, yellow and red, respectively, and helices H1 and H2 are grey. Elements are numbered in polypeptide-chain order. Numbers at the right indicate the first and last PDB residue represented in each displayed block. Insertions relative to Pol IV LF are left-aligned within shared gap slots for display; their placement does not establish residue equivalence between the other proteins. Pairwise structural statistics and sequence identities are reported in Table S2.
